# About

My name is Rick Peabody. I build systems, frameworks, and structures that help make sense of complex reality. After decades working in IT, operations, and strategy, I’ve learned that clarity isn’t something you find — it’s something you build.

This site is where I organize the work I’m doing now: mapping cognition, structure, incentives, and the patterns that shape how people think and act. I’m interested in the mechanics beneath behavior — the layers that sit under the surface of everyday life.

If you want to understand how I think, this site is the place to start. Everything here is a work in progress, but it’s grounded, structured, and built with intention.
