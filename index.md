# Structured Reality
## The Mobile Architect

A minimalist space dedicated to mapping the deep structure of reality — 
from the foundational domains of cognition, systems, and incentives, 
to the service‑layer dynamics that shape human interaction, 
to the surface‑level fields that emerge from them.

This is an evolving atlas of structural, service, and surface domains; 
a unified model of human cognition; and a framework for understanding 
the geometry beneath thought, behavior, and communication.
